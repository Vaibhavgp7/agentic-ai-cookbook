# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Shipping Options

### Standard Shipping (FREE for orders over $50)
- Delivery time: 5-7 business days
- Cost: $5.99 for orders under $50
- Tracking provided

### Express Shipping  
- Delivery time: 2-3 business days
- Cost: $15.99
- Tracking provided
- Saturday delivery available

### Overnight Shipping
- Delivery time: Next business day
- Cost: $29.99
- Must order before 2 PM for next-day delivery
- Not available for all locations

## Processing Time
- Orders placed before 2 PM ship same business day
- Orders after 2 PM ship next business day
- Weekend orders ship Monday
- Holidays may delay processing 1-2 days

## Tracking
- Tracking numbers sent via email within 24 hours of shipment
- Track on our website or carrier website
- Delivery confirmation required for orders over $200

## Shipping Restrictions
- We ship to all 50 US states
- PO Boxes accepted for standard shipping only
- APO/FPO addresses: 10-14 business days
- International shipping available to select countries

## Delivery Issues
- **Package Lost**: File claim after 10 business days
- **Damaged Package**: Refuse delivery or note damage on receipt
- **Wrong Address**: Contact us within 24 hours for address correction

## International Shipping
- Delivery time: 10-21 business days  
- Cost varies by destination
- Customer responsible for customs fees and import duties
- Some items restricted based on destination country regulations
