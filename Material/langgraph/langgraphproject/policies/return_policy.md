# E-commerce Return Policy

## Overview
Our return policy is designed to ensure customer satisfaction while maintaining fair business practices.

## Return Window
- **Standard Returns**: 30 days from delivery date
- **Electronics**: 14 days from delivery date  
- **Final Sale Items**: No returns accepted

## Eligibility Requirements
To be eligible for a return, items must meet the following conditions:
1. Item must be in original condition with tags attached
2. Original packaging must be intact
3. Product must not show signs of use or wear
4. Receipt or proof of purchase required

## Return Process
1. Contact customer support to initiate return request
2. Admin reviews and approves/rejects request within 24-48 hours
3. If approved, you receive return shipping label via email
4. Ship item back using provided label
5. Refund processed within 5-7 business days after receiving item

## Refund Methods
- **Original Payment Method**: Full refund to the card/account used for purchase
- **Store Credit**: Optional - receive 10% bonus as store credit instead
- **Shipping Costs**: Non-refundable except for defective items

## Exceptions
- Opened software, media, or digital products cannot be returned
- Personalized or custom-made items are non-returnable
- Items damaged due to misuse are not eligible for refund

## Defective or Damaged Items
If you receive a defective or damaged item:
1. Report within 48 hours of delivery
2. Provide photos of damage
3. We'll ship replacement immediately or issue full refund including shipping

## International Orders
- Return shipping costs are customer's responsibility
- Import duties/taxes are non-refundable
- Extended processing time (10-15 business days)
