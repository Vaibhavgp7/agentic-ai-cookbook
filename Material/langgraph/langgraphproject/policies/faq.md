# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Account & Orders

### How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package on our website by entering the tracking number, or directly on the carrier's website.

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, you can cancel orders that haven't shipped yet. Contact customer support immediately. Once an order is shipped, you'll need to follow our return process instead.

### How do I change my shipping address?
If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact customer support within 24 hours. Once shipped, we cannot change the address - you'll need to contact the carrier directly.

### What payment methods do you accept?
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Amazon Pay

## Returns & Refunds

### How long does a refund take?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your returned item. It may take an additional 3-5 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

### Who pays for return shipping?
For standard returns, customers pay return shipping costs. For defective or damaged items, we provide a prepaid return label at no cost to you.

### Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
Currently, we don't offer direct exchanges. Please return the item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want. This ensures you get your preferred item faster.

### What if my item is defective?
Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the defect. We'll immediately send a replacement or issue a full refund including original shipping costs.

## Product Information

### Are your products authentic?
Yes, all products are 100% authentic and sourced directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers.

### Do you offer price matching?
We offer price matching for identical items sold by major competitors. Contact customer support with proof of the lower price within 7 days of purchase.

### How do I know my size?
Each product page includes detailed size charts and measurements. If unsure, order your usual size or contact customer support for personalized assistance.

## Privacy & Security

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI-compliant payment processing. We never store complete credit card information on our servers.

### How do you use my personal information?
We use your information only for order processing, shipping, and customer support. We never sell your data to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

### Can I delete my account?
Yes, contact customer support to request account deletion. This will permanently remove your account and personal data from our systems within 30 days.

## Technical Issues

### The website isn't loading properly
Try clearing your browser cache and cookies. If the issue persists, try a different browser or contact our technical support team.

### I didn't receive my order confirmation email
Check your spam/junk folder. If still not found, contact customer support with your order details - we'll resend the confirmation immediately.

### Promo code isn't working
Ensure the code is entered correctly (case-sensitive), check the expiration date, and verify you meet the minimum purchase requirements. Some codes cannot be combined with other offers.
