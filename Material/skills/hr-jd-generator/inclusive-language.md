# Inclusive Language — Job Descriptions

## Replace biased phrasing

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| Rockstar, ninja, guru | Skilled, experienced |
| Young, energetic team | Collaborative team |
| Native English speaker | Strong written English for client communication (if required) |

## Do not include as requirements

- Age or graduation year as proxy
- Gender-coded traits ("aggressive", "dominant")
- College tier (IIT-only, tier-1 only)
- More than 8 must-have skills for mid-level roles

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Every must-have is genuinely required day one
- [ ] No good-to-have appears in must-have list
- [ ] Responsibilities describe work, not buzzwords only
