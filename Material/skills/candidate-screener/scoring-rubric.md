# Candidate Screening Score Rubric

## Must-have gate

For each must-have: Met / Partial / Not met.

If 2+ must-haves are Not met, cap final score at 4.

## Scoring (0–10)

| Component | Max points |
|-----------|------------|
| Must-have coverage | 6 |
| Good-to-have coverage | 2 |
| Practical fit (location, notice, domain) | 2 |

## Score bands

| Score | Typical recommendation |
|-------|------------------------|
| 8–10 | Shortlist |
| 6–7.9 | Shortlist or Hold |
| 4–5.9 | Hold |
| Below 4 | Reject or Hold (if HM may flex) |

Always state: `Score: X/10 — [must-have: Y/6] + [good-to-have: Z/2] + [practical fit: W/2]`
