# Interview Question Guide

## Good patterns

- "Walk me through a recent project where you built REST APIs. What was your ownership?"
- "Tell me about a production issue you debugged in a Spring Boot service."
- "Describe a sprint where requirements changed mid-way. What did you do?"

## Bad patterns — do NOT generate

- Age or graduation year as proxy
- Personal life (marriage, children, health, religion)
- Trick puzzles unrelated to the role
- "Culture fit" — use collaboration and delivery behaviour instead

## Balance

- 60% role-specific technical or domain depth
- 30% behavioural / delivery
- 10% motivation and role understanding
